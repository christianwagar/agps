Account Growth Propensity Scoring

A-GPS

Ranks a book of customer accounts by how ready each one is to grow. Golf-style: lower is better. Signals band 1–8, roll into five weighted buckets, and land in clusters P1–P4.

A framework, not a formula

The bands and weights here (ARR thresholds, win rates, renewal timing, bucket weights) were tuned for one book of business. Dial them in for yours: customer spend, deal sizes, risk appetite, sales motion, and data quality.

  1. Move the $ bands so your biggest customers score 1–2.
  2. Shift win-rate bands to what "good" means for you.
  3. Pick a renewal timing direction.
  4. Set bucket weights (they add up to 1.0).
  5. Down-weight patchy data.
  6. Rescore and sanity-check your P1s.
